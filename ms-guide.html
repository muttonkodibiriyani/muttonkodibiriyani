Writing a strong investment brief

State the business problem, evidence, target brands/markets, sponsor, budget, dependency map, and decision needed. Use quantified data wherever possible.

5-dimension scoring model

  • Financial Return Credibility 30%
  • Customer & Market Validity 25%
  • Technical & Delivery Credibility 20%
  • Strategic Alignment & Optionality 15%
  • Operational & Risk Credibility 10%

PASS is 75+, CONDITIONAL is 60-74, FAIL is below 60.

Bear case narrative

Write from the future after under-delivery. Name consumed budget, missed metric, root cause, and stop/pivot trigger.

Measurement standards

Every proposal needs a primary metric, baseline, target, data source, attribution method, owner, and review cadence.

Stage gate guidance

Stage funding follows 4%, 30%, 40%, 26%. Exit criteria must be observable, owned, and dated.

Financial policy

Maximum payback period is 36 months. Discovery funding is normally capped at $75K.

FAQ

Can a conditional pass proceed? Yes, with conditions captured and reviewed at the next gate.
